From the Director

We started OrchardsWood because we kept meeting bright children who had been written off by a system that moved at the speed of the class rather than the speed of the child. Every pupil here works through their material until they have genuinely mastered it — not until the term runs out.

That principle shapes everything: how we assess, how we report, and how we talk to parents. You will never receive a report from us that says your child is "average". You will receive one that says exactly which PACE they are on, what they have mastered, and which character traits we are working on with them this term.

How the curriculum works

OrchardsWood teaches the Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) curriculum and certifies through the International Certificate of Christian Education (ICCE).

01

Diagnostic testing

Every new pupil is tested to find the exact level they have mastered — regardless of the year group they arrive in.

02

Working through PACEs

Each subject is broken into workbooks. A pupil finishes one, is tested, and only then moves on.

03

Goals set with a supervisor

Pupils set their own daily goals on a goal card, which teaches planning long before they leave school.

04

ICCE certification

Certificates at General, Intermediate and Advanced level, benchmarked through UK NARIC.

From the Principal

A supervisor here is not a lecturer at the front of a room. They move between learning stations all morning, marking, checking goal cards, and catching the moment a child gets stuck — usually before the child says anything.

That is only possible because our learning centres are small. It is the single biggest reason our pupils close gaps quickly, and it is the thing I would most want a visiting parent to come and watch for themselves.
