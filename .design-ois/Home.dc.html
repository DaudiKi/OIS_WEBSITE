Why families choose OrchardsWood

Individual pace, not year groups

Each child works through PACEs at the level they have genuinely mastered, so nobody is pushed past a gap or held behind their ability.

Character taught, not assumed

Sixty desirable character traits are taught and reported on termly alongside academic marks — you see who your child is becoming, not just what they scored.

Qualifications that travel

ICCE certification is recognised through UK NARIC, so a pupil leaving OrchardsWood can continue their education anywhere in the world.
