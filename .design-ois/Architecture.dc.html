Backend architecture

One catalogue, one ledger

A PACE, a football club, a swimming slot, an e-learning module, a leadership course, a lunch plan and an event ticket look like seven different features. For billing they are one thing: something the school offers, that a student is given, which costs money and is delivered somehow. Model that once and every new offering — including ones nobody has thought of yet — bills correctly for free.

1 · Catalogue

What the school offers

One row per purchasable thing, whatever its kind.

2 · Assignment

What this pupil gets

An enrolment ties a pupil to an item for a term.

3 · Billing

What it costs

Every enrolment becomes an itemised line.

4 · Delivery

How it reaches them

At school, or provisioned in the portal.

The three that hang off the same spine

Events & tickets

A ticket type is a catalogue item of kind ticket. Issuing a ticket creates an enrolment, so a paid ticket lands on the fee statement exactly like swimming does — and a free one simply prices at zero.

Forms & surveys

A survey collects answers. A sign-up form does one thing more: on submit it creates an enrolment. That single rule turns "sign my child up for swimming" into a priced line without anyone re-keying it.
