Why this one breaks the pattern

Everything else in this system is deliberately traceable — you need to know exactly who owes what. A ballot needs the opposite, and it is the one place where storing the obvious thing is a mistake.

Two details that matter

Write both in one transaction. Otherwise a crash between them either loses a vote or lets a pupil vote twice.

Store a date, not a timestamp, on the vote itself. With 98 votes and exact times on both tables, matching them up by the clock is trivial — and the secrecy is gone.
